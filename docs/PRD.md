# CharityFlow — Product Requirements Document

## Overview
CharityFlow is the first all-in-one nonprofit operating system that replaces 6+ disconnected tools with a single platform built for people who don't know accounting.

## Target Users
Small to mid-size US nonprofits with annual budgets under $5M — including community organizations, churches, youth programs, food banks, animal shelters, and local foundations.

---

## Core Feature Engines

### 1. 🧾 CPA-Grade Tax Benefit Optimization Engine (P0)
- **Tax-Exempt Status Guardian** — Monitors activities that could jeopardize 501(c)(3) status
- **UBIT Tracker** — Identifies unrelated business income, calculates liability, auto-generates Form 990-T
- **Donor Tax Benefit Maximizer** — IRS-compliant acknowledgments, quid pro quo calculations, DAF tracking
- **In-Kind Donation Valuation** — FMV estimation, appraisal reminders for $5K+ gifts
- **Functional Expense Optimizer** — Auto-allocates expenses across Program/Admin/Fundraising
- **Employment Tax Shield** — Worker classification wizard, executive comp reasonableness
- **State & Local Tax Exemption Maximizer** — Sales tax exemption tracking, property tax renewals
- **Grant Accounting (ASU 2018-08)** — Conditional vs. unconditional revenue recognition
- **Form 990 Deep Optimization** — Optimal form version selection, public support test monitoring
- **Year-End Tax Planning Dashboard** — Tax season prep mode, projected savings, missing doc alerts

### 2. 🗺️ Location-Based Compliance Engine (P0)
- **Smart Onboarding** — Detects state/county/city at signup, generates personalized Compliance Roadmap
- **State-Specific Tax Calendar** — Auto-populates every filing deadline with countdown reminders
- **Regulatory Guidance Library** — Plain-language summaries of state nonprofit laws
- **Compliance Health Score** — 0-100% dashboard score tracking all compliance items
- **Year-Round Timeline** — Visual milestones across all 4 quarters
- **Local Grant & Funding Alerts** — Surfaces relevant government grants based on location
- **State-Specific Audit Prep** — Auto-generates audit-ready document packages
- **Quarterly Law Updates** — Pulls updated laws and guidelines every quarter via internet connection

### 3. 💰 Donor CRM & Fundraising (P1)
- Public donation portal with branded pages & shareable links
- Donor CRM tracking giving history & relationship notes
- Campaign manager with progress thermometers & social sharing
- Peer-to-peer fundraising
- Auto thank-you emails + IRS-compliant tax receipts in under 60 seconds
- Gift matching integration with corporate databases

### 4. 🎪 Local Event Success Engine (P1)
- **Smart Venue Finder** — GPS-based venue suggestions with nonprofit-friendly rates
- **Local Discount & Vendor Network** — Curated directory with savings estimates
- **Permits & Regulations Assistant** — Auto-detects required permits
- **Local Attraction & Culture Integrator** — Nearby events, local media contacts, cultural calendars
- **Event Marketing Toolkit** — Auto-generated flyers, neighborhood-targeted promos, QR codes
- **Event Budget Optimizer** — Pre-populated budgets, break-even calculators, ROI analysis

### 5. 📋 Board Governance (P1)
- Meeting scheduling with agenda builder
- Meeting minutes with auto-generated summaries
- Board voting and resolution tracking
- Document sharing and version control
- Board member directory with role-based permissions

### 6. 🔐 Audit-Grade Security (P0)
- Role-based access (Admin, Treasurer, Board Member, Staff, Volunteer)
- Smart notifications customized per role
- Immutable transaction logs for audit trail
- Bank-level encryption (AES-256)
- Two-factor authentication
- Notification preferences with do-not-disturb scheduling

### 7. 📊 Financial Management (P0)
- Plain-language transaction entry ("Money In" / "Money Out")
- Auto-categorization with bank feed integration
- Event-specific itemized tracking
- Quarterly report auto-generation
- Automated Form 990 generation and one-click filing

### 8. 🤝 Local Business Partnership Hub (P2)
- Sponsorship tiers (Bronze → Platinum) with clear benefits
- Sponsor impact reports showing ROI
- Community board for business-nonprofit matching
- Co-branded event pages + outreach templates

### 9. 🌐 Community Visibility & Growth (P2)
- Auto-generated public profile showcasing mission & impact
- Social media integration for milestone sharing
- Volunteer management with scheduling & hour tracking
- Grant Readiness Score
