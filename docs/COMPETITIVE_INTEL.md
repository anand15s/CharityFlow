## Key Insights

### QuickBooks Online Nonprofit
- **Strengths:** Widely known brand, TechSoup discounts ($80/yr for Plus), extensive integrations
- **Weaknesses:** Not built for nonprofits, requires accounting knowledge, no Form 990 automation
- **Recent:** Intuit phased out Desktop Nonprofit Editions, pushing users to cloud
- **Threat Level:** HIGH — brand recognition and market share

### Sage Intacct
- **Strengths:** True fund accounting, enterprise-grade reporting, AICPA preferred
- **Weaknesses:** $10K+/year minimum, $15-75K implementation, enterprise complexity
- **Threat Level:** LOW for our segment — priced out of small nonprofits

### Xero
- **Strengths:** Clean UX, affordable, global presence
- **Weaknesses:** No nonprofit-specific features, needs add-ons for everything
- **Threat Level:** MEDIUM — could add nonprofit features

### Aplos
- **Strengths:** Purpose-built for nonprofits, fund accounting, donation tracking
- **Weaknesses:** No automated Form 990, limited event management
- **Threat Level:** MEDIUM — closest competitor in segment

### Bloomerang
- **Strengths:** Best-in-class donor management, retention analytics
- **Weaknesses:** No accounting features, no tax automation
- **Threat Level:** LOW — complementary more than competitive
