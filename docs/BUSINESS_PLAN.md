# CharityFlow — Business Plan

## Executive Snapshot
- **Project:** CharityFlow
- **Vision:** To empower every nonprofit organization to manage their finances with confidence and ease.
- **Mission:** To simplify financial management for small nonprofits, enabling them to focus on their mission rather than paperwork.
- **Problem:** Nonprofits, especially smaller ones, struggle with managing finances, donations, expenses, and tax filings.
- **Solution:** CharityFlow provides an easy-to-use platform tailored for small nonprofits to manage transactions, automate donation tracking, expense management, quarterly reports, and Form 990 tax filing.
- **Target User:** Small to mid-size US nonprofits with annual budgets under $5M
- **Primary Region:** United States

## Risk Analysis

### Top Risks
1. **Competitive Pressure** (High) — Established players like QuickBooks
2. **Platform Security** (High) — Sensitive financial data requires top security
3. **Regulatory Compliance** (High) — Form 990 automation requires IRS compliance
4. **Workflow Integration** (Medium) — Diverse nonprofit operations
5. **Revenue Sustainability** (Medium) — Budget-constrained customers

### Mitigations
- Unique positioning on ease-of-use and affordability
- Regular security audits and incident response plans
- Legal expert consultation for continuous compliance
- User research to tailor integrations
- Flexible pricing with nonprofit tier discounts
