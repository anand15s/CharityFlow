# CharityFlow — One-Pager

## Project Name
CharityFlow

## Tagline
Simplifying nonprofit management and reporting

## Industry
Nonprofit Management Software

## Region
United States

## Problem
Over 200,000 small US nonprofits (under $500K budget) lack affordable tools purpose-built for their needs. They cobble together legacy accounting tools, spreadsheets, and manual processes to manage donations, expenses, tax compliance, donor relationships, events, and board governance — none of which talk to each other. This leads to missed tax deadlines, jeopardized 501(c)(3) status, poor donor retention, failed fundraising events, and audit nightmares.

## Solution
CharityFlow is the first all-in-one nonprofit operating system that replaces 6+ disconnected tools with a single platform built for people who don't know accounting. Core capabilities include:

1. **Plain-language transaction management** with auto-categorization and bank feeds
2. **Automated Form 990 generation** and one-click tax filing
3. **CPA-Grade Tax Benefit Optimization Engine** — protects tax-exempt status, tracks UBIT, optimizes functional expense ratios, maximizes donor tax benefits
4. **Location-Based Compliance Engine** — auto-detects state/county/city regulations at signup, personalized compliance roadmap, Compliance Health Score
5. **Donor CRM** with fundraising campaigns, peer-to-peer fundraising, auto-generated tax receipts
6. **Local Event Success Engine** — venue discovery, vendor discounts, permit guidance, event ROI analysis
7. **Board meeting management** — minutes, voting, document sharing
8. **Role-based access** with smart notifications, audit-grade security, immutable transaction logs

Every feature uses plain language — 'Chart of Accounts' becomes 'Money Categories,' 'Reconciliation' becomes 'Match Your Bank.'

## Target User
Small to mid-size US nonprofits with annual budgets under $5M — including community organizations, churches, youth programs, food banks, animal shelters, and local foundations.

## Business Model
B2B Subscription — $79-199/month tiered pricing

## Vision
To become the default operating system for every nonprofit in America.

## Mission
To democratize nonprofit management by giving every small organization the same CPA-grade financial tools, compliance intelligence, and fundraising capabilities that only large nonprofits can afford today.
